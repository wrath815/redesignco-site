What RedesignCo does

Four ways to fix a website that's costing you customers.

Full site redesign

A ground-up rebuild of your public pages — new layout, new copy, new mobile experience — that keeps what already works and fixes what doesn't.

  • Clear structure & navigation
  • Rewritten, plain-English copy
  • Handoff you actually own

Mobile & speed rescue

Most local traffic is on a phone. If your site pinches, zooms, or takes six seconds to load, we make it fast and thumb-friendly without a full rebuild.

  • Responsive, mobile-first layout
  • Compressed images & fonts
  • Core Web Vitals cleanup

Landing pages that convert

A focused page built around one job — a promotion, a service, a seasonal offer — with a single clear call to action and copy written to earn the click.

  • One goal, zero clutter
  • Forms & booking that work
  • Built to plug into ad campaigns

Findability & local SEO basics

The unglamorous fundamentals that help people actually find you: sensible titles, structured data, accurate hours, and a site Google can read.

  • Titles, meta & headings
  • Business schema & sitemap
  • Faster pages Google favors
